## Fed posture

The Federal Reserve voted 8-4 on April 29 to hold the federal funds rate at **3.5%–3.75%** — third consecutive hold. The 4 dissents (most since Oct 1992) reflect internal disagreement. Chair Powell's last day is May 15; Kevin Warsh is the leading successor candidate (hawkish, Senate confirmation pending). Market pricing: zero cuts in 2026, one 25bp cut in December 2027. The transition introduces policy uncertainty independent of the rate level — growth stocks (META, MSFT, NVDA) are most exposed to tightening-bias tail risk. Strait of Hormuz closure threat (global oil supply choke point) is a live left-tail scenario. Additional flashpoints: US-Venezuela tensions, NATO posture in Greenland, residual tariff uncertainty.
